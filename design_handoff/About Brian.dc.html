Hold me to it

Three questions to ask before you hire any LinkedIn expert — including me.

01

Can you demonstrate measurable results?

Ask for real campaign examples: the target audience, acceptance rates, response rates, qualified leads, meetings booked, and business generated.

02

Can you explain your strategy?

How an ideal client profile gets selected, how a profile gets optimized, how content and relationships get built, how messaging gets tested and conversion improved.

03

Can you explain LinkedIn's rules?

Invitation limits, platform policies, and the real risk of unauthorized automation. LinkedIn prohibits scraping and automated-engagement tools — a genuine expert works within that.
